## 1. Why Classic 1998 (N64) & 2011 (3DS) Booklets Are Only ~90% Accurate

Nintendo rebuilt *Ocarina of Time* from the ground up for Switch 2 rather than porting the N64/3DS code. While total counts for **Pieces of Heart (36)** and **Gold Skulltulas (100)** remain faithful, roughly **10% of specific locations, item mechanics, prices, and side systems changed**:

| Category | N64 (1998) / 3DS (2011) | Switch 2 Remake (Nov 2026) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Empty Bottles** | **4 Bottles** total | **5 Bottles** total! Added a brand-new Child Bottle in the **Market Potion Shop** (catch the escaped Golden Bug) + cut the **Big Poe Bottle** requirement in half from 1,000 pts (10 Poes) to **500 pts (5 Poes)** (1,000 pts gives a 200-Rupee Gold Rupee). |
| **Wallets** | 99 → 200 → 500 Rupees | **200 → 500 → 1,000 Rupees** (Kokiri Wallet starts at 200; 10 Skulltulas = 500 Adult's Wallet; 30 Skulltulas = 1,000 Giant's Wallet). |
| **Great Fairies (All 6)** | Free upon playing *Zelda's Lullaby*; DMT = Spin Attack, Castle = Din's Fire | **Now charge escalating Rupees (1,175 total)**: 25 → 50 → 100 → 200 → 300 → 500 Rupees! **DMT Summit (25)** and **Hyrule Castle (50)** swapped rewards: Summit grants **Magic Meter + Din's Fire** immediately; Castle grants **Charged Spin Attack**. |
| **20-Skulltula Reward** | Stone of Agony / Shard of Agony (grotto detector) | **Echo Gem** — equipable Gold Skulltula radar using dynamic glow, audio cues, and HD Rumble 2 (grottos now have subtle visual surface cues). |

## 3. All 5 Empty Bottles (4 Child + 1 Adult)

1. **[NEW] Market Potion Shop (Child — Immediate):** Enter the Market Square Potion Shop, talk to the panicked shopkeeper, catch the escaped Golden Bug on the floor with his loaner bottle, and keep the Bottle + free Red Potion.
2. **Kakariko Village 7 Cuccos (Child — Immediate):** Toss all 7 scattered Cuccos into Anju's pen during the day.
3. **Lon Lon Ranch Super Cuccos (Child — After Waking Talon):** Pay Talon 10 Rupees inside the farmhouse and find his 3 Super Cuccos in 30 seconds.
4. **Lake Hylia Sunken Bottle (Child — Silver Scale):** Win the Silver Scale in Zora's Domain, dive in Lake Hylia for Ruto's Letter, and show it to King Zora.
5. **[UPDATED] Castle Town Poe Collector (Adult — Epona + Bow):** Hunt **5 Big Poes** in Hyrule Field on Epona (500 points total, down from 1,000!) and sell them in the ruined Guardhouse. *(Selling all 10 for 1,000 points rewards a 200-Rupee Gold Rupee.)*

## 5. New Switch 2 Cross-Era Puzzles & Relocated Heart Pieces

Because the **Stone of Agony** was replaced by the **Echo Gem** and dungeon Heart Pieces were eliminated, Nintendo replaced 3 N64 Heart Pieces with 3 new overworld puzzles:

1. **Hyrule Field — Northern Moat "Sell somethin', will ya?" Scrub Grotto (`hp-05`):**
   - **Adult Link:** Inspect the Fairy's Thought carved on the lonely tree near the ruined Castle Town moat to learn the password `"Sell somethin', will ya?"`.
   - **Child Link:** Bomb the cracked rocks on the grass bank east of the drawbridge moat, enter the grotto, give the Business Scrub the password, and pay **100 Rupees**.
2. **Hyrule Field — Western Red Canyon Time-Locked Chest (`hp-06`):**
   - **Adult Link:** Under the red rock plateaus right before Gerudo Valley, inspect the looted chest and read the glowing **Fairy's Thoughts** wall scribble promising to unlock the chest *in the past*.
   - **Child Link:** Travel back to the past and open the unlocked chest!
3. **Death Mountain Crater — Left Ledge Business Scrub (`hp-21`):**
   - **Child Link:** Enter Death Mountain Crater from the summit, drop onto the lower left rock ledge before heat drains your hearts, hit the Business Scrub with the Slingshot/Shield, and buy the Piece of Heart for **100 Rupees**.
4. **Hyrule Field — SE Peahat Wetland Gold Skulltula (`gs-010`):**
   - **Child Link:** Bomb the cracked stone near the Peahats in SE Hyrule Field, read the fairy wall message, and slay the ordinary Skullwalltula inside.
   - **Adult Link:** Return to the grotto in the future to find a **Gold Skulltula** in its place!
